# Photos

All 57 photos are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and fall under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no attribution required. Each was picked by eye for its slot, resized to at most 1400 px on the long side (the heroes to at most 2200 × 1500 px) and compressed.

Every trade has its own folder in `public/images/`. The trade files in `src/data/trades/` reference them by name (`hero`, `team`, and one photo per service or reference project). A missing file shows a dark placeholder, so the site still looks finished.

## Replacing a photo

1. Save the new image under the same name in `public/images/<trade>/`. Aim for 1400 px on the long side and under ~300 KB.
2. For a real client, swap `hero.jpg`, `team.jpg` and the reference project photos for their own work first: they carry the most trust.

## Map data

`src/data/towns.json` lists 2 610 Slovak towns, villages and city districts with coordinates. It was built from the GeoJSON in [drakh/slovakia-gps-data](https://github.com/drakh/slovakia-gps-data), which is based on OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/)).
